Another Bag of Chips is a retro video game album containing 10 royalty-free chip/SNES-styled music tracks. It is a follow-up collection to the original "A Bag of Chips". This collection contains a wide range of moods and tones, music from Another Bag of Chips is suited to fit a variety of different scenarios and use-cases.
